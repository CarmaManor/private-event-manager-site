Invitation Only

Invitation-only event management
for private groups

A platform for private clubs, membership organizations, and invitation-only groups that need to control who joins and who attends. Hosts curate the member list; guests see only what they've been invited to.

Process

If a host has invited you to apply
here's what happens next

Every profile on the platform is reviewed and approved by a host before any access is granted. There is no public registration.

01

Submit Application

Complete the application form with your details. No account is created until a host approves you.

02

Host Review

A registered host reviews your application. Unapproved applications are automatically deleted after 3 days.

03

Approval & Access

Once approved, you receive a secure link to complete your profile and access the platform.

04

Receive Invitations

Hosts invite you to their events. You manage RSVPs and communications privately within the platform.

Alternatively — hosts may send you a direct email invitation. Clicking the link in that invitation takes you directly to a profile completion form. The host has already approved your access.

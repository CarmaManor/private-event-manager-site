About the Platform

Built for events that
require privacy

Private Event Manager is not a public platform. There are no discovery features, no public profiles, and no searchable directory. Every attendee is personally vetted and individually invited.

Organizers maintain full control over who attends their events, how guests communicate, and what information is shared. Guests receive invitations only when a host explicitly includes them.

"Access is granted by invitation or application review — not by registration. Every profile on this platform has been personally approved."

Process

How access is granted

There are two paths to join the platform. Both require host review and approval before any access is granted.

01

Submit Application

Complete the public application form with your details. No account is created until approval.

02

Host Review

A registered host reviews and vets your application. Unapproved applications are automatically deleted after 3 days.

03

Approval & Access

Upon approval you receive a secure link to complete your profile and access the platform.

04

Receive Invitations

Hosts invite you to specific events. You manage your RSVPs and communications privately.

Alternatively — hosts may send you a direct email invitation. Clicking the link in that invitation takes you directly to a profile completion form. The host has already approved your access.

Platform Features

Designed for complete control

Every feature is built around the principle that private events require private infrastructure.

◈

Invitation-Only Access

No public registration. Every profile is personally approved by a host before access is granted.

◎

Guest Management

Hosts control exactly who attends, with granular RSVP management, waitlists, and capacity controls.

◇

Private Communications

In-platform messaging between hosts and guests. Event chat rooms visible only to invited attendees.

◉

SMS Notifications

Optional SMS notifications for event invitations, reminders, and updates. Explicit opt-in required.

◈

Host Networks

Organizers maintain private networks of trusted guests across multiple events over time.

◎

Location Privacy

Event locations can be revealed only to confirmed attendees, after RSVPs, or after a deadline.
